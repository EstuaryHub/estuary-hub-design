Design proposal · 26 Sep 2026

Design foundations

Where fresh water meets salt: a cool, watery ground, one tidal teal for action, and a warm silt held back for moments of welcome. Nothing shouts.

Values live in design/tokens/tokens.css. Contrast ratios are WCAG 2.1, against the background named.

PRIVACY PROMISE · built into every contact pattern

No email address or phone number is ever shown.

Every "contact" button opens a short form. The message is passed on; the visitor never sees the leader's address, and the leader chooses whether to reply.
