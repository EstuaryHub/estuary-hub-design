What happens in an Estuary group

Open to anyone

For anyone who wants to explore a topic deeply with others. No membership, no agenda to sign up to.

Listening first

Participants practice listening well and articulating their own thoughts — speaking, but also hearing.

Across every line

Deep connection across ideological, philosophical, cultural and demographic lines — in the room and beyond it.
