What is Estuary?

A discussion group, open to anyone

Estuary is a type of discussion group which is open to anyone who seeks to deeply explore topics with others. The group is a hospitable, welcoming space in which participants practice listening well, articulating their thoughts, and engaging in respectful, authentic dialogue.

[Photo: a group in conversation]
[Photo: participants listening]

Why participate in an Estuary group?

Estuary has the power to build deep connections among different people across ideological, philosophical, cultural, and all demographic lines. It encourages participants not only to speak, but also to listen.

In the give and take of the conversation, participants practice active listening, building empathy and understanding, paving the way to build a community which is relationally engaged both within the context of the conversations, but also in the world.
