# Estuary Hub — Discovery Interview Guide

Sep 26, 2026 · @joseph · One page, for whoever holds the conversations · Before the Static MVP build

## Purpose

Every persona and flow in these documents was worked out from the current website, not from talking to anyone. Five 30-minute conversations test the assumptions that would change the plan if they are wrong. **Listen, don't pitch.** Ask about what people actually did, not what they think they would do.

## What we are testing

| # | Assumption | If it is wrong | Best heard from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Leaders will claim and maintain their own listing** | App MVP has no users ([P9](../decisions/open-questions.md#p9-what-does-success-look-like-for-each-release--blocking-for-static-mvp-build)) | Leaders |
| 2 | Newcomers look for "a group near me" first, and mostly arrive from a search engine | The home page and search are pointed the wrong way | Recent seeker, site admin |
| 3 | Listing changes are frequent and painful enough to need self-service | App MVP can wait, or shrink to the ELSNet pipeline | Site admin, leaders |
| 4 | Applicants suffer from not knowing where their application stands | The ELSNet pipeline is lower priority | ELSNet coordinator |
| 5 | Leaders want their email address off the site, and will accept a contact form | The re-consent message and S5 need rethinking | Leaders |

## Who to talk to

Two group leaders (one active, one whose group is quiet or has stopped), the person who administers the site today ([F5](../decisions/open-questions.md#facts-somebody-needs-to-go-and-find)), the ELSNet coordinator, and one person who went looking for a group in the last year.

## Opening (all five)

"We're redesigning the Estuary Hub site and want to understand how it's actually used before we change anything. There are no right answers, and nothing you say will be quoted with your name. Can you tell me about the last time you used the site, or dealt with anything to do with it?"

## Questions

**Leaders**
- Walk me through the last time something about your group changed: time, place, format. What did you do about the listing?
- How do people usually first get in touch with you? What happened with the last few?
- Your email address is on the site today. Has that caused you anything, good or bad?
- If we sent you an email asking you to confirm or claim your listing, what would you realistically do with it? When did you last act on an email like that?
- What would you most want to be able to do yourself, without asking anyone?

**Site administrator**
- Walk me through last month: what did you actually do for the site, and how long did it take?
- Which requests come in most often? Which are the most annoying?
- What do you do now when a listing is wrong? How do you find out?
- Is anyone using the Blog, Groups or Chat apps? The member search? ([F2](../decisions/open-questions.md#facts-somebody-needs-to-go-and-find))

**ELSNet coordinator**
- Walk me through the last application, from the form arriving to that person being listed.
- Where does it stall? What do applicants ask you about?
- How many are in progress right now, and how do you keep track of them? ([F3](../decisions/open-questions.md#facts-somebody-needs-to-go-and-find))

**Recent seeker**
- How did you first hear about estuary conversations? What did you do next?
- How did you look for one? What did you type, and where?
- What did you need to know before deciding to go? What nearly stopped you?
- If there had been nothing near you, what would you have done?

## Closing (all five)

"Is there anything about the site we should definitely not lose? Is there anyone else we should talk to?"

## Handling notes and findings

- **Keep notes out of this repository.** It is public. Hold them wherever the organisation keeps private files, and delete them once findings are written up.
- Write up findings without names or identifying detail, in a short table: assumption → what we heard → how many of five → what changes in the plan.
- Take the findings to the product owners, and update the roadmap, the function lists, or [Open Questions](../decisions/open-questions.md) wherever an assumption did not hold.
- One conversation is an anecdote. Act on a pattern: two leaders saying the same thing, or the administrator and a leader describing the same pain.
